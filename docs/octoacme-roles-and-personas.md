# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing leads define and execute quality standards, testing strategies, and acceptance validation. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and maintain quality gates throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and QA approach during planning
- Create and maintain test plans and acceptance criteria validation
- Execute manual QA and coordinate automated testing
- Report quality metrics and blockers in weekly syncs
- Participate in release readiness reviews
- Document known issues and post-release verification results
- Collaborate with developers on test design and testability

### Goals
- Ensure zero critical defects reach production
- Maintain test coverage and observability standards
- Reduce cycle time through clear acceptance criteria

### Typical Communication
- Quality standups and sprint planning
- PR reviews for testability and test completeness
- Weekly quality reports and metrics
- Release readiness meetings and sign-off

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Partner on test automation, acceptance criteria clarification, and test coverage goals
- **Product Managers:** Validate acceptance criteria and feature specifications before testing
- **Project Managers:** Report quality blockers and release readiness status
- **Technical Lead:** Align on testing architecture and test strategy for complex components

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and project sponsors provide business context, prioritization guidance, and approval authority. They champion the project and ensure alignment with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and business case
- Participate in initiation and planning gates
- Provide regular feedback on progress and outcomes
- Approve scope changes and trade-off decisions
- Escalate blockers to leadership when needed
- Champion project within the broader organization
- Ensure project aligns with organizational strategy

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers on business objectives
- Minimize scope creep and timeline slippage
- Maintain executive visibility and alignment
- Enable resource and organizational support for delivery

### Typical Communication
- Milestone approvals and decision gates
- Monthly stakeholder updates and executive briefings
- Scope change requests and priority adjustments
- Escalation channels for business-impacting blockers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers:** Receive status updates, approve scope changes, escalate organizational blockers
- **Product Managers:** Provide business priorities, success metrics validation, and market feedback
- **Developers:** Review progress demonstrations and validate solutions meet business intent

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical leads establish technical direction, make architectural decisions, and guide the team on technical trade-offs and best practices. They ensure scalable, secure, and maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Make technology choices and tool decisions
- Review technical designs and code for architectural alignment
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Mentor developers on technical excellence and best practices
- Lead technical design reviews and spike investigations
- Ensure compliance with security, performance, and scalability standards

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable, and secure technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and complexity
- Build for future extensibility and reusability
- Accelerate team velocity through clear technical direction

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review comments on architectural alignment
- Spike planning and investigation guidance
- Risk mitigation discussions in weekly syncs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Provide technical guidance, design reviews, and mentorship on best practices
- **Project Managers:** Identify technical risks and dependencies; guide estimation and timeline
- **QA/Testing Lead:** Align on testing architecture and complex test scenarios
- **Product Managers:** Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs between features and quality

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters/Agile coaches facilitate ceremonies, remove process blockers, and coach the team on agile principles and continuous improvement. They enable sustainable team velocity and psychological safety.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives
- Track velocity and identify process improvements
- Remove impediments and blockers at the team level
- Coach team on agile practices and Definition of Done
- Monitor team health and psychological safety
- Track action items from retrospectives and ensure follow-up
- Adapt processes to team needs and organizational context

### Goals
- Enable sustainable team velocity and predictability
- Foster continuous improvement culture
- Maintain team engagement and reduce burnout
- Build psychological safety and blameless learning

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- One-on-one coaching conversations
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Team health and process improvement discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers:** Partner on schedule planning, blockers removal, and stakeholder communication
- **Developers:** Coach on agile practices, help remove blockers, maintain team morale
- **All Roles:** Facilitate ceremonies, capture action items, and drive continuous improvement
